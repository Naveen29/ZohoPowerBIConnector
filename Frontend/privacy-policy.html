Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Zoho Power BI Connector collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use the service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account information such as name, email address, and password.
  • Zoho configuration data required to connect your Zoho account.
  • Contact messages submitted through the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate access to your dashboard and Zoho connector.
  • Send transactional messages, such as email verification and notifications.
  • Respond to support or contact requests.

3. Data Security

We protect your data using appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. Sensitive information such as passwords and API credentials are stored securely and are not shared with unauthorized third parties.

4. Cookies and Tracking

This site may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience. Cookies are used to maintain session state and to provide a more seamless login and dashboard experience.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to send email messages, process analytics, or host application assets. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their services and are required to keep your data confidential.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting support through the website.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a revised date.
