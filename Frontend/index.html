Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about the connector.

What does the connector support?
+

It supports Zoho Analytics data retrieval, Power BI dataset refresh, and subscription-based access.

Can I use it with Power BI Pro?
+

Yes. The portal is compatible with Power BI Desktop and Power BI Service accounts.

How secure are credentials?
+

Zoho client secrets and refresh tokens are encrypted in the database and never exposed in plaintext.

Is there a free trial?
+

Yes, the Pro plan includes a trial period so you can evaluate the connector before committing.

Can I update Zoho settings later?
+

Yes. The portal allows credential updates and revalidation at any time.

What happens if my plan expires?
+

Expired plans will block plugin validation until the subscription is renewed.

How do I contact support?
+

Use the contact form below or access the support page once logged in.

Does it support multiple users?
+

Yes, the portal is designed for customer management and can support multiple licensed users per account.

Is documentation available?
+

Yes, documentation pages are included for privacy policy, terms, support, and usage guides.
